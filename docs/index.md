# Nexiqon documentation

Nexiqon lets you put assistants in front of your customers and staff. The assistants answer from
your own documents with citations, use your systems through tools you control, and hand over to a
person when they should. It runs as a shared cloud service, as a hybrid (your data stays in your
cluster), or entirely on your own servers, from one product.
