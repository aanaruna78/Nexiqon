# Getting Started with Acme Cloud

Acme Cloud is a workspace for teams to manage projects, files and automations.

## Create your workspace

Sign up at the Acme website with your work email. The first person to sign up becomes the workspace owner.
Choose a workspace name and a region. The region decides where your data is stored and cannot be changed later.

## Invite your team

Open Settings, then Members, and select Invite. Enter one email address per line.
Invitations expire after 7 days. Owners and admins can resend an expired invitation.

### Roles

There are four roles: owner, admin, editor and viewer. Viewers can read projects but cannot edit them.
Only owners can delete the workspace or transfer ownership.

## Install the desktop app

The desktop app runs on Windows 10 or later, macOS 13 or later, and Ubuntu 22.04 or later.
Download it from the Downloads page and sign in with your workspace URL.
