# Billing and Plans

## Plans

Acme Cloud has three plans: Free, Team and Enterprise. The Free plan includes up to 3 members and 2 GB of storage.
The Team plan costs 12 dollars per member per month. Enterprise pricing is agreed with our sales team.

## Invoices and payment methods

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing month and sent to the billing contact.
We accept credit cards and, on the Enterprise plan, bank transfer. You can download past invoices from Settings, Billing.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time
and keep access until the end of the paid period.

## Cancel your subscription

Owners can cancel from Settings, Billing, Cancel plan. Your data is kept for 60 days after cancellation and then deleted.
