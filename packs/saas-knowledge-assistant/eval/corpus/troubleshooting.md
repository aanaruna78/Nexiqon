# Troubleshooting

## Sync is stuck

If the desktop app shows Sync paused, check your internet connection and free disk space.
Signing out and back in clears the local cache. Files larger than 5 GB are not synced.

## Cannot sign in

Reset your password from the sign-in page. If your workspace enforces SSO, sign in through your identity provider instead.
Accounts are locked for 15 minutes after 10 failed sign-in attempts.

## Email notifications are missing

Check the Notifications page in your profile and make sure your mail server allows messages from notifications at acme example.
Digest emails are sent once a day at 8 in the morning in your time zone.
