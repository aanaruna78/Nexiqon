# Data Export and Retention

## Export your data

Owners can export all workspace data from Settings, Data, Export. The export is a ZIP archive with JSON files and attachments.
Large exports are prepared in the background and a download link is emailed when ready. The link is valid for 7 days.

## Retention

Deleted projects stay in the trash for 30 days and can be restored by admins. After 30 days they are permanently deleted.
Audit logs are retained for 1 year on Enterprise and 90 days on other plans.

## Data residency

Workspaces can be hosted in the EU, the US or India. The region is chosen when the workspace is created.
